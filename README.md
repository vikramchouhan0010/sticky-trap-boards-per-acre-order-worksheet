# Sticky Trap Boards per Acre: Order Worksheet

A worksheet for turning an advisory figure into a sticky board order. Replace the planning assumptions with your own advisory and the figures on your quotation.

## Method

1. Take the boards per acre from your crop advisory. NIPHM, India's national plant health institute, lists yellow sticky traps at 10 an acre among whitefly measures in tomato, and blue traps at about 5 an acre for thrips.
2. Decide how often a board is replaced (example: every 15 days) and the crop length (example: 120 days).
3. Add a spare share (example: 5%) and round up to packs (example: packs of 10).

## Worked example, open field

| Area | Yellow boards | Blue boards | Hanging at one time | Whole season | Packs of 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 acre | 10 | 5 | 15 | 126 | 13 |
| 2 acres | 20 | 10 | 30 | 252 | 26 |
| 5 acres | 50 | 25 | 75 | 630 | 63 |
| 10 acres | 100 | 50 | 150 | 1,260 | 126 |

These are planning assumptions, not BLUSTAL specifications. Polyhouse density differs from open field.

## What to send for a quotation

Area, crop, field or polyhouse, boards per acre from your advisory, replacement interval, pack count wanted.
